01

Privacy-First Web Tools

Sixteen small tools I kept needing. Shrinking files, converting formats, cutting the background out of a photo, stripping EXIF data, merging PDFs, pulling the vocals out of a song. They all run inside the browser tab. Your files never get uploaded, because there's nowhere for them to go.

TypeScript, React, WebAssembly

02

Buddy

A small orb that sits on top of your other windows. Say “Hey Buddy” and it starts listening. Talk over it and it stops. The model, the voice and the speech recognition are all bundled into the app, so there's no account to make and no key to paste in, and it keeps working with the wifi off.

Built with
Electron, llama.cpp, Kokoro TTS, Whisper
Runs on
macOS, Windows, Linux
Download Buddy→

03

Vela

A browser with the tracking taken out. Ad and tracker blocking is on before you open the first tab, there's a local AI assistant in the sidebar, and your history and bookmarks sit in one file on your machine that you can open and read yourself. It makes two kinds of request: the pages you ask for, and one check for a new version.

Built with
Electron, TypeScript, Chromium
Sends
The pages you open, and one update check
