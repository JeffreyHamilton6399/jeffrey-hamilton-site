How I got here

The egg business

The first thing I ever ran was selling eggs. That was the whole enterprise, and it is on here because it is honestly where the line starts.

I hadn't built anything yet. Most of that stretch went on hanging out with friends. Nothing on this page existed, and it would be four years before any of it did.

Cedar Hills

In 2018 I lived in Cedar Hills, and soccer was my sport. Then COVID hit, and we moved to Lindon in the middle of it.

Making friends after a move is hard as a kid, and harder when nobody can go anywhere. I kept to myself and watched a lot of Disney Channel and YouTube. In 2022 I started a channel of my own. I deleted most of those early videos. They were bad.

I taught myself Blender, cut videos for YouTube, and built little games for the plain fun of getting something on screen to move the way I meant it to.

It is where I learned to finish a thing and put it out, which turned out to be the part that mattered.

Lindon to Provo

Lindon is where things picked up. I made a lot of friends there, and I still have them. We don't hang out much now, but I could message any of them and set something up.

Swimming was my main sport, mostly for the fun of it with friends. Out of the pool I got into game dev and wanted to make things in Blender.

None of it was for anyone, which turned out to be the point.

Swimming, Blender and a lot of new schools

I switched schools a bunch of times around then.

In middle school we moved to Provo, into the house I still live in.

2024 is where it turned. I started leaning on AI to build faster than I could alone, and the first real target was Minecraft mods: small tools for a game I already knew inside out.

It was the first time code stopped being homework and started being the fastest way to get an idea out of my head.

Growing out of it

Middle school in Provo was rough. I fell in with a friend group that wasn't a good influence, and I didn't always make the best choices.

Eventually I realized staying with them was a bad idea, and I grew out of it. Around then a friend from Lindon got me into karate, and I'm still doing it.

Karate, and freshman year

In 2024 I started high school and found the friend group I still hang out with.

I hadn't found my main sport yet. Karate is the closest so far.

What I'm aiming at

I want to make something that lasts. Not an app that just exists, but one people genuinely use and enjoy, and that is still worth talking about years from now. If it puts my name in the history books, even better.

Along the way, a lot more languages: HTML, Python, whatever the next thing needs.

Outside the code, I want to enjoy life to the fullest and come out of it with connections I did not have before. When my name comes up, I want the people who know me to say they love me.

Now

Where the hand is

Egg money to shipped software in seven years, most of it self-taught: Blender and game engines first, then Minecraft mods with a language model wired into them, and now web and desktop apps people actually use. TypeScript and Next.js day to day, Electron when it needs to be a real program, Java for the mods, and enough Blender and Revit left over to be useful. Next I want to build the on-device kind: software that runs the model on your own machine, so the useful part happens without anything leaving it.
