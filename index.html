What I've built

The three I'd show you first.

01

Privacy-First Web Tools

Sixteen small tools I kept needing. Shrinking files, converting formats, cutting the background out of a photo, stripping EXIF data, merging PDFs, pulling the vocals out of a song. They all run inside the browser tab. Your files never get uploaded, because there's nowhere for them to go.

Built with
TypeScript, React, WebAssembly
Size
16 tools, all in the browser

02

Buddy

A small orb that sits on top of your other windows. Say “Hey Buddy” and it starts listening. Talk over it and it stops. The model, the voice and the speech recognition are all bundled into the app, so there's no account to make and no key to paste in, and it keeps working with the wifi off.

Built with
Electron, llama.cpp, Kokoro TTS, Whisper
Runs on
macOS, Windows, Linux
Download Buddy→

03

Slate

A whiteboard a few people can draw on at the same time, with a 3D editor built into it. Edits show up live for everyone, and you can talk to each other while you work. It installs like a normal app and keeps a copy of your boards on your machine, so the server going down doesn't cost you anything.

Built with
TypeScript, Yjs, WebRTC, Fastify
Does
2D and 3D editing, voice chat, offline

Other things

Hover to stop on one.

  • Vela A browser that doesn't collect anything. Ad and tracker blocking is built in, there's a local AI assistant in the sidebar, and everything it saves goes into one file you can open and read yourself. Electron · TypeScript
  • InboxPilot Connects to your Gmail, sorts what comes in, and writes drafts that sound like you wrote them. You host it yourself and bring your own API key. TypeScript · Gmail API
  • Bulletin Classifieds for wherever you live. You can post without making an account, replies come through a relay so your email stays hidden, and old listings clear themselves out after a month. Next.js · Prisma
  • SignBridge Point your webcam at ASL fingerspelling and it reads the letters back. It runs in the tab, so no video gets sent anywhere, and it tells you how sure it is instead of pretending. TypeScript · On device ML
  • Line Glide Draw a line and watch a guy sled down it, like Line Rider. There are levels to get through, and the physics is all written by hand. Next.js · Canvas
  • PixelParty Make a room, send the link to someone, and draw pixel art together. Nobody has to sign up for anything. TypeScript · WebSockets
  • Counterpoint An AI that argues with you on purpose. Handy when you want the holes poked in an idea before somebody else finds them. TypeScript · LLM
  • SpellFall A word game where everybody plays at once. Spell fast enough and you stay in; the rest get knocked out until one person is left. TypeScript · Multiplayer

About

I like making things that just work. No fluff, no broken buttons, no excuses.

I'm Jeffrey Hamilton. I'm 17, and I've been working since 2018.

These days I split my time between teaching karate at Bobby Lawrence, helping out at Day's Market, building small tools that run in the browser, and making YouTube videos. None of it came from a bootcamp or a fancy program. I just kept showing up and figuring things out.

I like the kind of work where you can point at something at the end of the day and say you made it. Most of what's on this page started because I wanted it to exist and couldn't find a decent version, so I built one.
