The short version

None of this started with code.

  1. 2018

    Eggs

    Four years of farm operations at the family egg business. It is not complicated work. You do it every morning whether or not you feel like it, and if you skip a day the problem is still there tomorrow, only bigger.

    That is most of what I learned about finishing things.

  2. 2022

    The second test

    I failed my first black belt test. Went back, trained, and passed the second one. Then I spent three years teaching at Bobby Lawrence Karate, which is where I found out that explaining a thing properly is harder than doing it.

    That is roughly how the rest of it has gone too.

  3. Since then

    Things I couldn't find

    Every project below started the same way: I wanted something, went looking, and what I found wanted an account, a subscription, or my files on its server first. So I built the version I wanted instead.

    None of it came from a bootcamp or a fancy program. I just kept showing up and figured things out.

  4. 2026

    Now

    Software engineering at Provo CAPS, interning at Doing Business Intelligently, managing an apartment on 200 East, and on the register at Day's Market. Senior year, diploma expected 2027.

    I like making things that just work. No fluff, no broken buttons, no excuses.

What I've built

Three of them up close

01

Privacy-First Web Tools

Sixteen small tools I kept needing. Shrinking files, converting formats, cutting the background out of a photo, stripping EXIF data, merging PDFs, pulling the vocals out of a song. They all run inside the browser tab. Your files never get uploaded, because there's nowhere for them to go.

Built with
TypeScript, React, WebAssembly
Size
16 tools, all in the browser
Open the toolbox→

02

Buddy

A small orb that sits on top of your other windows. Say “Hey Buddy” and it starts listening. Talk over it and it stops. The model, the voice and the speech recognition are all bundled into the app, so there's no account to make and no key to paste in, and it keeps working with the wifi off.

Built with
Electron, llama.cpp, Kokoro TTS, Whisper
Runs on
macOS, Windows, Linux
Download Buddy→

03

Vela

A browser with the tracking taken out. Ad and tracker blocking is on before you open the first tab, there's a local AI assistant in the sidebar, and your history and bookmarks sit in one file on your machine that you can open and read yourself. It makes two kinds of request: the pages you ask for, and one check for a new version.

Built with
Electron, TypeScript, Chromium
Sends
The pages you open, and one update check

Everything else

Fourteen more, turning

01 / 14 Drag it, or keep scrolling
  • MOUSE FX Sixteen mouse and cursor effects — trails, custom cursors, springs, click bursts. Each one is a single HTML file you can copy straight into your own site. Vanilla JS · Canvas
  • Violet Hour A dark violet theme for VS Code and Visual Studio 2022. Every color comes out of one palette file that the build runs 113 contrast checks over. VS Code · Color theme
  • Slate A whiteboard a few people can draw on at the same time, with a 3D editor built into it. Edits show up live for everyone, and a copy of every board stays on your machine. Yjs · WebRTC
  • Robotics Builds VEX robots put together for Robotics 1 and 2. Tank tracks, flywheels, and a lot of taking things apart again after they broke. VEX · Robotics
  • InboxPilot Connects to your Gmail, sorts what comes in, and writes drafts that sound like you wrote them. You host it yourself and bring your own API key. TypeScript · Gmail API
  • Bulletin Classifieds for wherever you live. Post without making an account, replies come through a relay so your email stays hidden, and old listings clear themselves out. Next.js · Prisma
  • Minecraft Mods Four Fabric mods and modpacks on Modrinth, about 1,200 downloads between them. Chat macros, keybind profiles, a performance pack, and a tab list tweak. Java · Fabric
  • School Animations I make animations. These are a few of the logo idents I made for my school, including the one that plays on TBird TV. Motion graphics
  • SignBridge Point your webcam at ASL fingerspelling and it reads the letters back. It runs in the tab, so no video gets sent anywhere, and it tells you how sure it is. TypeScript · On device ML
  • Line Glide Draw a line and watch a guy sled down it, like Line Rider. There are levels to get through, and the physics is all written by hand. Next.js · Canvas
  • PixelParty Make a room, send the link to someone, and draw pixel art together. Nobody has to sign up for anything. TypeScript · WebSockets
  • CAD Housing A full set of construction drawings for a house, done in Revit for my architecture class. Floor plans, elevations, sections, roof and foundation. Revit · Architectural design
  • Counterpoint An AI that argues with you on purpose. Handy when you want the holes poked in an idea before somebody else finds them. TypeScript · LLM
  • SpellFall A word game where everybody plays at once. Spell fast enough and you stay in; the rest get knocked out until one person is left. TypeScript · Multiplayer
